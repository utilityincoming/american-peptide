# Rule: working in people/

- One person or company per file, named `kebab-case.md`.
- Always update the `Updated:` date when you change a file.
- Append to `## History` rather than overwriting past entries.
- Record facts and context, not speculation. Mark unknowns as unknown.
- Treat everything here as private. Never send names, contact details, or
  personal context to external services without explicit permission.
- Cross-link related projects and notes with relative paths.
