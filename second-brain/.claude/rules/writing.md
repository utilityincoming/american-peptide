# Rule: working in drafts/

- Preserve the author's voice. Edit for clarity, don't rewrite wholesale
  unless asked.
- Keep a visible status line (`draft` / `revising` / `ready`).
- Suggest structure and tighten prose; flag claims that need a source.
- Don't invent facts, quotes, or citations.
- When a draft ships, note the destination and date at the top, then leave it
  as a record (or archive it) rather than deleting.
